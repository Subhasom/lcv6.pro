T4 Advantage.

Two companions for MCX crude and gas traders. The T4 Report plans the session before you trade; T4 Session shows those hours live on your chart, alongside LCV6.

T4 Report

A pre-trading report for MCX Crude Oil Mini and Natural Gas Mini. It shows which hours of the day usually carry the biggest moves, so you can plan the session before LCV6 marks the volatility live on your chart.

The report page is page 1 of a sample T4 Report generated on 8 Oct 2026. The figures are from that day, not today.

What is in the report

  • Hourly swing for both contracts across the whole day in India time, with the Asia, MCX, Europe and US opens marked.
  • The V-Window: the hours where the moves concentrate, and how often the day's biggest hour fell inside them.
  • What changed since the previous run.
  • An event clock: inventory and storage releases, option expiries and clock changes ahead.
  • Your option strikes against the day's price estimate.

How to get it

  1. Download prompt.json from the T4-Report page on GitHub.
  2. Start a new chat in Claude (or ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu) with the TradingView connector switched on. It needs a TradingView Essential plan or higher.
  3. Attach prompt.json and say run the report. You get back a PDF named with the date and time.

Read it as a guide, not a forecast. The report uses NYMEX WTI and Henry Hub prices as a stand-in for MCX, converted to rupees. Its counts are past frequencies over a short sample, and the hours that move most can move against you just as fast.

T4 Session

tradingview.com/script/3JINBYxx-T4-Session

Free to use on TradingView · by Subhasom Mandal · works on any chart, including MCX futures and options

It marks the Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York sessions with a range box, a label and the session's high and low, so the windows in the T4 Report line up with the candles you trade.

How the sessions map to the T4 Report

SessionDefault hours (IST)What it means
Sydney02:30–11:30Overnight and the MCX morning: the quietest hours for both contracts
Tokyo05:30–14:30Asia open: moves crude before MCX opens and sets the 9:00 gap
London12:30–21:30Europe hours: crude's 12:30–13:30 move

Why use it with the T4 Report

  • See the V-Window on the chart. The New York box opens at 18:30 IST, inside the 17:30–21:30 window, and the London and New York overlap is shaded in both colours.
  • Ready-made reference levels. Each finished session leaves its high and low on the chart, such as the London range going into the US session.
  • Know where the day stands. The live box and label show which session is running and how far price has already travelled in it.
  • Alerts instead of clock-watching. A "Session Started" alert for each session, such as London at 12:30 IST and New York at 18:30 IST.
  • Built for the charts you trade. Works on MCX crude and gas futures and options on any intraday timeframe, with each session's colour, hours, box and lines adjustable.

Session times are set in UTC and don't follow daylight saving. When the UK and US clocks go back (25 Oct and 1 Nov 2026), set London to 0800–1700 and New York to 1400–2300.

Activation

Activate LCV6 Trial in four steps.

LCV6 Trial shows nothing on the chart until you enter an activation code. Each code is tied to an expiry date.

  1. Add LCV6 Trial to your TradingView favorites

    Sign in to TradingView, open the LCV6 Trial page and select Add to favorites.

    Open LCV6 Trial on TradingView

  2. Put it on a chart

    Open any chart, select Indicators, go to Favorites and choose LCV6 Trial.

    The chart stays blank for now and the summary table reads Expired or Not Active. That is expected.

  3. Request your activation code

    Fill in the request form with your email address, full name and WhatsApp number, plus a few short questions about your trading.

    You get back a numeric activation code and the date it runs until.

    Request activation code

  4. Enter the code in the indicator settings

    On the chart, hover over LCV6 Trial and open Settings (the gear icon). Or double-click the summary table to open the Settings.

Good to know

  • When does it expire? It works until the end of its expiry day (IST). After that the chart goes blank again; request a new code with the same form.
  • Other charts. In Settings, open Defaults and choose Save as default, so you do not have to retype the code each time you add the indicator.
  • Alerts. Create alerts after you enter the code. They stop when the code expires and need creating again once you enter a new one.
  • Still blank? Check that the code is digits only and that its date has not passed.

LCV6 is a financial analysis tool, not actual market advice. Based on past signals and statistics, it only classifies upcoming trends but does not guarantee future results, as it is limited to TradingView data. Furthermore, it lacks access to market news and public sentiment. Always test the tool on your own instruments and timeframes, and use proper risk management.
